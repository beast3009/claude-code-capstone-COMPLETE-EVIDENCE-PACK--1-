# Supply-Chain Briefing

## Executive Summary
The reference workflow identifies the principal supply-chain risks, ranks affected areas, and produces recommended follow-up actions.

## Key Findings
1. Supplier concentration is the primary exposure.
2. Lead-time variability is the second major risk.
3. Inventory coverage provides partial mitigation.

## Recommended Actions
- Review alternate suppliers.
- Reassess safety-stock thresholds.
- Monitor lead-time variance.
